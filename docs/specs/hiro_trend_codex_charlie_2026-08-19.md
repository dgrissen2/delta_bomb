## Codex Strategy Review — Generic Logic
**Verdict**: FAIL (12 findings)

### Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Category | Details |
|---|---------|----------|----------|---------|
| 1 | The endpoint does not measure an executable delta-bomb fill | HIGH | Mechanics | A future SPX touch of 3, 5, or 7 points does not establish that the second option leg could be filled for zero/credit. Option value also depends on delta, gamma, IV, skew, decay, bid/ask spread, and latency. The experiment needs historical option quotes or a conservative executable-pricing model, with the exact strikes and expiration selected at the first-leg timestamp. |
| 2 | Path ordering and actual risk rules are omitted | HIGH | Risk | “Fill within 60 minutes” and “adverse >15” are reported independently, so a path can hit the stop first and the desired level later yet count as a success. Score first passage among second-leg fill, the stated sale+3.5 stop, timeout, and any long-leg exit. The sale+3.5 rule is not mapped to option-price data, while the long-first sequence lacks an equally precise loss rule. |
| 3 | Minute observations are treated as independent opportunities | HIGH | Statistics | The 33 UP minutes and 7 eligible DOWN minutes arise from only three days and likely from a few continuous trend episodes. Overlapping 15/30/60-minute outcomes make adjacent rows nearly duplicates. Effective sample size is the number of independent episodes or sessions, not 40 minute rows. Results require episode deduplication, session-clustered uncertainty, and leave-one-day-out reporting. |
| 4 | The experiment is not pre-registered and the sweep is data-mined | HIGH | Falsification | The primary endpoint, direction pooling, minimum lift, tolerated adverse rate, and stopping rule are unspecified. Thousands of threshold combinations were swept on the same five sessions. The best UP cell’s +6.1-point lift is therefore selection-biased—especially when 1,788 eligible cells averaged −14.4 points and the reported maximum was only +6.1. Freeze one specification before collecting additional sessions and reserve later sessions as untouched validation data. |
| 5 | Key state definitions are not reproducible | HIGH | Computability | `W`, `cons`, `r2`, `ddn`, `mag`, `cp`, `nx`, `pc`, and sweep column `p` lack complete formulas, units, sampling frequency, session-reset rules, and bar alignment. It is unclear whether EMA operates on cumulative HIRO or increments, whether agreement concerns levels or slopes, how drawdown is normalized, and how price confirmation is computed. The unexplained `p=8.0` also conflicts with the stated pullback/bounce threshold of 3 points. |
| 6 | Leak-free timing is not demonstrated | HIGH | Methodology | With five-second HIRO observations mapped into one-minute starts, the signal at minute \(t\) must use only provider observations received before the executable entry timestamp. R², running extremes, pullback depth, synchronized price, and minute OHLC can all leak information if the full current minute is used. Specify timestamp availability, aggregation, one-bar lag, and whether entry occurs at the next quote or next bar open. |
| 7 | The controls do not isolate HIRO’s incremental value | HIGH | Controls | Because the HIRO state requires price confirmation, the relevant control is the identical price-defined candidate set with HIRO omitted, evaluated at the same timestamps. The shown price-only control uses different thresholds and populations, while the meaning and matching procedure for each “base” are unstated. Compare nested, paired signals on identical candidate minutes, and include time-of-day, day, pullback depth, volatility, and gamma-regime matching. |
| 8 | The first run is adverse to the UP thesis and inconclusive for DOWN | HIGH | Conclusions | The primary UP cell underperforms its baseline at every displayed horizon and distance—for example, +5 points within 30 minutes is 6.1% versus 41.8%. DOWN shows promising +3-point results but has only seven eligible rows across three days; its +5 and +7 results are mixed. EMA-only results show the same asymmetry. The defensible reaction is “sample evidence against the UP formulation; DOWN hypothesis-generating only,” not general support for smooth HIRO continuation. |
| 9 | Apparent confirmations are mechanically dependent | MEDIUM | Signal Design | `nextExp` is an overlapping subset of `all`, so agreement is not independent confirmation. Call and put lines are components of Total, and price confirmation is partly embedded in selecting a trend. Treat these as features in a pre-specified model or ablation—not votes whose agreement automatically increases confidence. Require agreement of contemporaneous slopes or increments, not merely accumulated line levels. |
| 10 | Gamma-regime mechanics are not identified | HIGH | External Validity | All five sessions were positive-gamma sessions with narrow 19–38-point ranges. In that regime, options-driven pressure may be absorbed or damped; behavior can reverse under short gamma, high realized volatility, OpEx, or event risk. Either define the strategy as positive-gamma-only or pre-register interaction branches. Five homogeneous sessions cannot establish the claimed mechanism across regimes. |
| 11 | “SPX index only” is inconsistent with the signal’s instrument identity | MEDIUM | Scope | The traded instrument and price are SPX, but SpotGamma’s `S&P 500` HIRO aggregates SPX, SPY, /ES, and XSP. That basket can be a predictor for SPX, but the experiment cannot call it SPX-only flow or attribute the effect specifically to SPX options. The signal must be labeled as basket HIRO, with SPX-only referring solely to execution and outcomes. |
| 12 | Five to twenty sessions cannot validate a production rule | HIGH | Evidence | Five sessions can reject grossly poor variants, expose coding errors, and estimate signal frequency. Even twenty sessions are unlikely to cover sufficient regimes or independent episodes for stable tail-risk and adverse-excursion estimates. A 20-session gate can authorize continued paper testing only; production evidence needs an untouched out-of-sample period, enough independent episodes per direction, and session-clustered confidence bounds around both fill lift and loss rate. |

### Assumptions Audit

| Assumption | Classification | Why It Matters |
|------------|----------------|----------------|
| Smooth basket HIRO represents persistent executable dealer hedging | Unverified | HIRO estimates hedging pressure; it does not observe executed hedges, timing, or completion. |
| A smooth cumulative line reflects stable new flow | Fragile | Integration and autocorrelation can create high R² even when increments are noisy or fading. |
| HIRO continuation increases directional second-leg completion | Contradicted for UP in-sample | Every displayed primary UP fill comparison is below baseline. |
| DOWN performance is genuinely stronger than baseline | Indeterminate | Seven eligible rows across three sessions cannot distinguish a repeatable effect from one episode or day. |
| A 3–7-point SPX move produces a zero/credit spread | Unsupported | Spot movement alone does not determine option-leg execution economics. |
| Minute rows are separate trials | False | State persistence and overlapping outcome windows create strong dependence. |
| `all` and `nextExp` agreement supplies independent evidence | False | The scopes overlap by construction. |
| Call/put agreement has one unambiguous definition | Unsupported | Agreement could refer to level, increment, EMA slope, or flow classification; these are not equivalent. |
| Price confirmation plus HIRO is better than price confirmation alone | Unproven | No paired, identical-minute ablation establishes incremental HIRO value. |
| Positive-gamma findings transfer to other gamma regimes | Unsupported | The sample contains no regime variation with which to identify that interaction. |
| Basket HIRO can be described as SPX-only flow | False | The source signal includes SPX, SPY, /ES, and XSP. |
| Thresholds such as 0.7 consistency, 0.8 R², and 0.25 drawdown are economically grounded | Unsupported | No derivation, calibration rationale, or pre-registration is supplied. |
| Adverse spot excursion is an adequate proxy for leg risk | Fragile | Option P&L depends on volatility, skew, time, spread, and whether the adverse threshold occurs before the fill. |
| Twenty sessions can establish live-trading readiness | Unsupported | The likely number of independent signals and regime coverage remains too small for reliable performance or tail estimates. |
