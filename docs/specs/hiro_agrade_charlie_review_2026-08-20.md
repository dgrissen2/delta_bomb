2. Resolving the steep paradox

The missing variable is **flow acceleration conditional on price response**.

Steep is ignition when HIRO is accelerating and spot has not yet fully responded: positive second derivative of flow, shallow price impact, then expanding basket breadth. That is stored mechanical energy.

Steep is climax when flow remains high but acceleration rolls over, spot stops responding, or the run immediately suffers meaningful giveback. That is maximum effort, minimum result—exhaustion.

Build a state tag using:

- Current flow rate versus the prior 5–10-minute rate.
- Spot points gained per $1B of cumulative flow.
- Drawdown from peak cumulative flow.
- Realized-vol percentile and time of day.
- Distance to Call Wall as context, not a hard cutoff.

Call Wall proximity matters because steep buying directly beneath a positive-gamma ceiling can be absorbed; farther away, the same impulse has runway. But acceleration and price response should dominate.
