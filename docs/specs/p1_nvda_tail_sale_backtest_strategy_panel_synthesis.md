| 7 | The buyback and fill model is structurally biased. An allegedly immediate resting order begins only the next session; close-ask observations miss intraday and opening fills; and automatic limit fills omit quote duration, size, queue position, and live-order state. | 8 | The recent 2026 intraday sample cannot validate fill behavior across the 2023–2025 regimes or determine whether earlier regime results change sign. | 13 | Promotion gates are arbitrary and underdefined. | 14 | The design cannot isolate incremental signal or conversion edge and cannot estimate ruin risk from the short sample. ### Disagreements Or Tensions

- Split normalization was rated CRITICAL by the Senior Quant Researcher and HIGH by Generic Logic and Charlie McElligott; the synthesis preserves the highest severity.
- Promotion-gate defects were rated CRITICAL by Generic Logic and Charlie McElligott but HIGH by the Senior Quant Researcher; the synthesis preserves the highest severity.
- No substantive directional conflict exists among the reviewers; Charlie uniquely emphasizes flow-archetype separation, while the Senior Quant Researcher uniquely flags the cooldown and completed-spread inventory interaction.
