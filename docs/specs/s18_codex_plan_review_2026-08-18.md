## Codex Plan Review — Verdict: FAIL (8 findings)

| # | Finding | Severity | Dimension | Details |
|---|---------|----------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Classifier statistics are unsupported and mislabeled | MEDIUM | Spec fidelity | §18.0 reports OOF AUC 0.52–0.61 and 0.78–0.86 by noon, but none of the supplied source documents reports these models or values. §18.1 then calls AUC “accuracy,” which is a different metric. Cite the missing model output or remove the numbers and use the correct metric consistently. |
| 2 | Underlying touches are presented as bomb completions and retraces | HIGH | Logic error | The touch artifact contains underlying `up_hit`, `dn_hit`, `alt`, and `mae_up`, not option quotes or fills. At 4 bp, one-way up-touch is 80.14%/86.15% and down-touch is 78.01%/83.83%, while the documented up-then-down event is only 56.30%/67.59%. Thus “4 bp completes,” “the retrace is the 85% event,” and the memo’s “move and then come back” are unsupported; for the round-trip measure, the second hour adds about 11.3 percentage points, not 6. A spot touch also cannot establish a `sale − 0.10` option fill without delta, IV, spread, and quote data. |
| 3 | An unmeasured placement hypothesis is promoted as an operating rule | HIGH | Internal consistency / Test adequacy | §18.0 calls exhaustion placement “confirmation” and an empirical rule, and §18.1 calls all four rules evidence-backed, but the decision paragraph admits the trigger has not been measured for this trade. Worse, it says the rule remains even if the proposed test finds no improvement, so the experiment cannot falsify adoption. Keep exhaustion placement labeled as a hypothesis and predefine the trigger, baseline, minimum effect, confidence/OOS requirements, and accept/reject consequence. |
| 4 | The source mechanisms and normalization are inaccurately summarized | MEDIUM | Factual accuracy | The uptrend source describes 10–12-point pullbacks as approximately `2.5u`, where `u = max(0.10 × IM, 4 points)`, not 2.5 opening-range spreads. The CIO memo also attributes every failed ride rule to those pullbacks, whereas the below-VT source attributes the clean-rule failure primarily to entering 60% through the range with only 33 bp left; its three approaches were churn, hold, and wait-for-clean, not three ways of waiting for a clean trend. The quoted alligator verdict also omits its closing words, “not following,” despite the verbatim framing. |
| 5 | Live trading rules contain undefined or unsourced contracts | HIGH | Data contracts / Edge cases | The supplied toolkit does not define a “5-bar typical-price mean/VWAP,” Put-Wall probe, live-efficiency thresholds of 0.5/0.3, or the memo’s “hard price cap.” `path`, the cap amount, rule precedence, missing-level behavior, and trigger timing are unspecified. There are also no guards for zero/invalid `CW−VT`, zero `IM`, zero path, NaN bars, or absent SG levels. These omissions make the proposed naked-leg rules neither reproducible nor safely executable. |
| 6 | The CIO memo changes band position from order selection into a trade veto | MEDIUM | Memo fidelity | The toolkit says high band position/low Call-Wall room implies a capped, fade-expected up-move; its explicit trade veto is the untested VT-close/Call-Wall confirmation. §18.0 uses band position to prefer long-first over sell-first, but §18.1 says gamma-band position vetoes whether to leg at all. | 7 | Adverse-tail and sizing claims overgeneralize the available data | HIGH | Logic / Units | The 17% and 7% figures are specifically 120-minute, 4-bp `mae_up` rates; the artifact has no corresponding down-oriented MAE, so they cannot support both order directions or “every loss.” Moreover, 20/40 bp are not fixed 15/30-point moves throughout a 2022–2026 sample. A static 21-delta estimate for 30 SPX points is `30 × 0.21 × 100 = $630`, not $800; an $800 estimate requires an explicit gamma/vega/repricing model. |
| 8 | Touch-table provenance has an incorrect endpoint | MEDIUM | Data provenance | The document says the 845-session touch sample ends 2026-06-30, but `spx_touch_stats_full.parquet` contains 845 distinct days through 2026-07-10. The referenced raw real-SPX store has 853 days through 2026-07-10, so the document should distinguish the 853 raw days from the 845 eligible touch days, state the eight exclusions, and correct the endpoint. |
