## Path rules

- **Buy-first puke:** RR Rank >=60, call-skew rank (CS) <=40, IV Rank <=65, drawdown <=-8%, and negative five-day return. Buy the complete cheap spread now.
- **Buy-first standard:** Good/Better/Best cheap-call surface plus constructive technical/relative strength. Buy the ~15-delta call first, then sell the adjacent upper call into strength.
- **Sell-first grab:** RR Rank <=10, front call-wing rank >=85, kink >=70, positive five-day return, within 5% of the 20-day high, IV Rank 30-70, and no near earnings. Sell the far call first, then bid for the nearer call; keep a mandatory breakout stop.
- **RR/IV relationship:** RR/CS/wing ranks identify which side of the surface is rich or cheap. IV Rank says whether the whole surface is expensive or cheap versus its own year; it strengthens sizing/selection but is not the directional signal by itself.
