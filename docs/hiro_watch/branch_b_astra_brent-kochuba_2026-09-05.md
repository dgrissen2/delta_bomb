## Codex Strategy Review — Brent Kochuba (Synthetic Gamma & Microstructure)
**Verdict**: FAIL (6 findings)

### Persona Lens

This lens notices the exposure **before** the second leg fills. B attempts to acquire downside protection by first selling downside exposure. Positive HIRO does not establish that dealer positioning will cushion a decline, and an intended exit is not a guaranteed loss limit. The relevant question is whether acquiring the finished spread justifies the risk taken while waiting.

### Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Category | Details |
|---|---------|----------|----------|---------|
| 1 | The financing step creates the vulnerability the finished position is meant to protect against. | HIGH | Structural risk | Selling the lower-strike put leaves B short downside convexity until the higher-strike purchase fills. That purchase requires the higher put to become cheaper than the earlier sale price: B needs favorable repricing before protection exists. A decline or volatility increase can instead make protection more expensive while damaging the short. This exposure is structural to sell-first; capacity merely determines whether it is taken. Flow gates cannot turn an unpaired short into a protected spread. |
