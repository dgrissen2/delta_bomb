## Codex Plan Review — Charlie McElligott
**Verdict**: FAIL (5 findings)

| # | Finding | Severity | Dimension | Details |
|---|---------|----------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | The basic trade economics are broken | HIGH | Economic viability | The baseline spent $1,630 on failed attempts to earn only $160 from completed bombs—roughly $10 lost for every $1 captured. A 52% fill rate is not success when incomplete trades carry losses vastly larger than the completed credits. This should be the document’s blunt lead conclusion. |
| 2 | Open inventory disguises the realized damage | HIGH | Risk presentation | The reported −$880 MTM combines −$1,470 cash losses with +$590 from 16 open bombs. Those bombs are one concentrated downside position, not sixteen independent wins, and their mark swung from +$1,265 to +$165 on one rally. Relative candidate improvements driven by open marks are unstable and should not be described as fixes. |
| 3 | The tape has confirmed almost nothing | HIGH | Validation | Confirmation contains one trading session. September 3 showed only that two shallow-flow Branch-A signals lost $420 during an 80-point rally and that the −4 gate avoided those trades. It did not confirm that deep negative flow works, because no qualifying deep-flow trade occurred. September 4 adds no evidence because the engine stood down. |
| 4 | The apparent Branch-A solution is discovery-selected and regime-confounded | HIGH | Signal validity | The −4 threshold looks attractive because five discovery trades all completed, but they came from only three falling sessions—the exact tape most favorable to downside-flow setups. The −1 discriminator already failed its stated test. The evidence therefore supports “shallow signals are harmful,” but not yet “−4 is a durable edge.” |
| 5 | Neither Branch B nor the wider credit target repairs the engine | MEDIUM | Strategy mechanics | Branch B is too scarce to offset Branch A, and relaxing its safeguards produces more trades but larger losses, indicating the filters are blocking bad flow rather than suppressing a proven opportunity. `credit030` preserves the same losing A attempts and reduces B completions. The current evidence says these variants rearrange exposure; they do not solve the adverse-selection and legging-loss problem. |
