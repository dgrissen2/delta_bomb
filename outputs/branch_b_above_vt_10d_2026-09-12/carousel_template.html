Rules, timing and data

Common rules: EMA5 > EMA9 > EMA20, both adjacent gaps widening, total EMA5−EMA20 fan ≥ 0.10 ATR, green signal candle, and close no more than 1.50 ATR above EMA5. The original VWAP condition is omitted from both SPX price-only versions. ADX is display only.

Thrust: close at least 10 basis points above the highest candle body of the preceding three bars (or the available two at the session start); basis points use the current close as denominator.

Staircase: an additional admission: three rising closes, the two prior candles green with bodies larger than their combined wicks, close at/above the prior body-top, and EMA5 rising at least 0.60 ATR over two bars. A bar passing both is labeled thrust.

Timing: entry decisions 10:00–14:30 ET inclusive. Candles are labeled by assumed interval start; a 09:55–10:00 signal becomes available at 10:00, with the stored 10:00 one-minute open as its reference. The cache does not retain enough timestamp metadata to independently certify start/end labels; no executable fill or return is asserted.

One marker per run: each version emits the first qualifying bar and rearms after any failed condition. Staircase can start or bridge a run, so it can also suppress a later thrust marker. The mode selects the rule package; entry-type checkboxes filter its displayed entries and dates without recalculating rules. “All qualifying bars” adds small circles at signal closes, including repeats of the selected types, within the entry window.

Dates: the underlying sample is the latest ten complete cached sessions through September 11, 2026 whose 09:30 open is strictly above same-date VT. The carousel and date selector show only sessions with entries matching the current mode and selected entry types. All ten remain in the research data. No intraday VT or SG-index gate. Averages and ATR use earlier available regular-session history, including intervening below-VT days.

Scope: entries only; fixed-time baseline skipped. No HIRO, volume, VWAP substitute, exits, option simulation or threshold search. Ten dates are a visual prototype, not an accuracy estimate.
