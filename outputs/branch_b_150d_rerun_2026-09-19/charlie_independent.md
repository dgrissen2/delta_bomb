## Claude Strategy Review — Charlie McElligott
**Verdict**: CONDITIONAL PASS (8 findings)

### Persona Lens
This lens treats the reversal as a problem of speed and regime first, and only then as a signal problem. Getting +5 within 60 minutes is a bet on realized velocity, so a regime change shows up mostly as more timeouts ("neither"), not more adverse outcomes. The lens also sees that falling spot-ATM IV during an up-move is partly mechanical. As spot rises, ATM rolls to a strike with lower IV, and spot and vol normally move in opposite directions. So the IV signal may partly restate the price move rather than add new information. VT is a model level, not measured dealer gamma. No positioning or flows are claimed below.

Most likely. **(b)** Slower 2025 markets (35-minute opening range 21.7 vs 27.1; B06 10:00-hour win rate 0.56 vs 0.83, with added 2026 at 0.82). This is a hypothesis. It is falsified if qualifier status is not explained by the pre-breakout underlying price change already in the stored data. **Unknown:** actual dealer gamma and flows. |

| 8 | Warmup runs across gaps in the SPX cache. | LOW | data integrity | The cache has only 5 sessions in March 2025, 6 in April 2025 and 4 in March 2026. **What must stay fixed:** the +5 before −15 rule within 60 minutes from the next minute's open; same-minute touches counted as ambiguous; all events and zero-event dates kept; the eight rule modules unchanged; no IV, range, time, cooldown or target thresholds; and no tuning after scoring. The timeout breakdown, same-date B01 contrasts and hour/cohort tables explain the shift. None of them may become entry filters.
