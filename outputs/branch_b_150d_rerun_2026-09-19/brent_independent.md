## Claude Strategy Review — Brent Kochuba (Synthetic Gamma & Microstructure)
**Verdict**: CONDITIONAL PASS (7 findings)

### Persona Lens

A gamma and microstructure reviewer asks one question first: how far can the tape actually move in 60 minutes? That question sits ahead of "which filter works." Three things follow that a generic reviewer might miss:
- **"Above VT" is only a label for the regime.** It comes from a single preopen number. It is not a measurement of dealer gamma.
- **Most of the hit rate comes from range.** "+5 before −15" is a race between two fixed distances. When the tape moves less, more signals end as "neither", whatever the signal was.
- **The sector-IV filter tells a vol-compression story.** That story only holds if the quotes are precise enough to tell whether IV was really speeding up on the way down.

This sample contains no dealer gamma, CTA or flow data. Every "pinning" or "dampening" idea below is therefore a hypothesis, not something measured.

### Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Category | Details |
|---|---------|----------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Hit rate is not edge, and the B01 control is not used as the comparator | HIGH | evaluation-design | **Measured mechanics:** the target is one-third the distance of the stop, so any drift-free path that reaches a barrier hits the target first about 75% of the time. Differences between cohorts mostly show up as changes in the "neither" rate. **The gap:** B01 is listed as one row in the table but is never used as the comparison. **Fix:** make the main contrast for each family a same-date, hour-matched difference against B01, weighted to match that family's mix of entry hours, with bootstrap by date. Report the adverse rate next to it. Otherwise the smaller 2025 range will look like a family success or failure. |
| 2 | Explanation 1 (most likely): the IV uplift is a winner's-curse result, not a 2025 regime effect | HIGH | diagnosis | **Measured, from `event_ledger.csv`:** within the added 2026 dates, original-IV qualifiers hit 29/50 (58.0%), versus 64.4% for non-qualifiers in the same cohort and 64.6% on the qualifiers' own dates. In 2025: 47.5% vs 54.6%. In the original 50: 72.0% vs 61.2%. The reversal also survives the continuously-above-VT restriction (39/58 vs 76/151), so crossing VT does not explain it. **Interpretation:** the original 50 had been searched repeatedly, and the sign of the acceleration could not be resolved within the quote envelope. **What would overturn this:** a paired-date uplift interval above zero in the added 2026 dates. With about 50 events that is unlikely, and the data shows no evidence of uplift rather than proof of harm. **Protocol:** state that the IV overlays will not be attached to B01–B10. |
| 3 | Explanation 2: the drop in plain B06 is more "neither" outcomes, not more losses | MEDIUM | diagnosis | **Measured:** in the added 2025 dates, "neither" rose to 37.1% (vs 22.5%) and adverse fell to 10.9% (vs 13.6%). Opening range was 21.7 vs 27.1 points. The +5 target is 7.43 bp at the 2025 median SPX of 6,728, vs 6.95 bp at 7,193. The weakest hour is the 10:00 hour: 2025 hits 56% (n=88) vs 82–83% in both 2026 cohorts. **Hypothesis:** a quieter, mean-reverting tape. **Contrary evidence:** the one proxy available, open-to-VT cushion, was smaller in 2025 (median 28 vs 34.5/51.4 points), so "sitting deeper in positive gamma" is not supported. **What would overturn this:** B01 falls just as much in 2025 (the whole tape was weaker), and the gap closes within fixed range strata (it was the mix of days). If the gap persists within strata, the cause is something else. |
| 4 | The diagnostic bins are not fixed in advance, so diagnostics can turn into a parameter search | MEDIUM | pre-registration | Opening range, ATR, distance above VT and hour have no declared cut points or units. **Fix:** freeze now. Use terciles of day-level values across all 150 dates, computed before any outcomes, in both points and ATR units. Report trends only; no bin becomes a rule. Label distance above VT as a proxy for the regime, not a measure of gamma size. |
| 5 | The "unchanged" rerun adds a new primary gate | MEDIUM | protocol-integrity | The 10-day dashboard only required the open to be strictly above VT. The new lockout ("all prior minute lows strictly above VT") was written after the B06 VT audit. It is mechanically reasonable, since a VT breach is the only regime-change signal available here. But it has to be declared as a change, with primary vs opening-only status fixed before outcomes. VT sits on a 5-point grid and some opens are only 0.4 points above it, so some lockouts will be one-tick wicks. Report lockout time and breach depth by cohort as description only, and do not tune on them. |
| 6 | The same IV filter is looser in 2025 because sector coverage differs | MEDIUM | measurement | **Measured:** qualifiers were 35.7% of 2025 parents, vs 24.9% in the original 50 and 22.3% in the added 2026 dates. The share of events with unknown state was 18%, 27% and 37%. The requirement is 6 of 11 sectors, so better coverage makes it easier to pass. The "same" rule is therefore less selective in 2025. **Check:** tabulate the qualifier rate by number of observed sectors, as description only with no new cutoff. |
| 7 | Sample scope and which comparisons can mislead | LOW | inference | 58 of the 66 2025 dates fall in Aug–Dec and the other 8 in Feb. Mar–Jul 2025 is missing because of the VT-corroboration requirement, which removes exactly the stressed, below-VT months where the worst paths live. Conclusions apply only to days that opened above VT with a matching preopen note. **Comparisons that will mislead:** (a) the pooled 150 hit rate vs the original 50; (b) treating original-50 vs added-100 as train vs test for B01–B10. The 10 dashboard design dates do not overlap the 150 (verified 0/10), so for every family except B06 the split is by era, not in-sample vs out-of-sample. (c) Ranking families on hit rate without the B01 contrast. Also state before outcomes that full-prehistory warmup is primary, and that the 10-day reproduction only checks the code. |

**Keep fixed:** the 150 dates, the +5/−15/60-minute score with ambiguous and "neither" left in the denominator, the frozen module hashes, the existing 14:30 cutoffs in the recipes, the independence of the IV overlay, and results reported per cohort. **Allowed diagnostics:** the B01-paired contrast, range and hour strata frozen before outcomes, and descriptive tables of lockouts and coverage. None of these may produce a new threshold.
