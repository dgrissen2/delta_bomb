# B09 opportunity expansion: three tests, one volume comparison

Completed September 20, 2026. **2025–September 18, 2026; same 239 research dates;
causal above-VT only; +5 before −10 within 60 native minute bars.** No spacing is
the primary policy. Every experiment starts from the same B09 sector F4 OR SPX
reference; the three expansions were not combined with one another.

## What worked, partly worked, and did not work

**All three proposals expanded raw N at roughly the existing observed accuracy.**
Five-minute IV persistence added the most signals. B07 added the most active dates.
These results support further consideration, not a statistically demonstrated
guarantee that accuracy is unchanged. The intervals for changes permit both
improvement and deterioration.

**The extra opportunities are not equally independent.** Most persistence additions
are near existing signals, and its advantage weakens with chronological spacing.
For all three approaches, added signals on entirely new dates look weaker than
additions on already-active dates. Those subgroups are small and descriptive.

**Relative volume did not improve the current B09 OR selection.** High and ordinary
volume had almost identical target rates; a high-volume gate would discard many
winners. No inverse-volume rule or new volume-based admission route was adopted.

## What exactly did five-minute persistence mean?

For entryT, inspect already-computed endpointsT−5 throughT−1. At one common
earlier endpoint, at leastfour identical sectors must have M>1 and negative
recent IV slope. Those same sectors must still have valid, negative IV slope at
T−1. SPX has the equivalent one-instrument route. Either route qualifies.
All current qualifiers remain included. Missing current readings remain unknown.
There is no stale-quote fill, future confirmation, changed MAD scale, loosened
threshold, or aggregation of different sectors' bursts at different times.

The174 additions split into90 sector-only persistence confirmations,78 SPX-only
confirmations (including15 with unknown sector persistence), andsix confirming
through both routes. This is a count decomposition, not a new route-selection test.
The witness endpoint and all sixty instrument/endpoint records perB09 entry are
preserved for verification. The five-minute duration was fixed before outcomes.

## SPY relative volume: no useful improvement in the current selection

RVOL is five completed minutes of Nasdaq-venue SPY share volume divided by the
median same-clock five-minute volume over the60 preceding source-calendar
sessions. RVOL>1 is high; no threshold tuning. Missing current or historical
observations are not zeros, and all60 historical windows must be complete.

The cache supports2,766/3,141 parent features. Another235 entries have no current
date available after the cache's June11,2026 endpoint;140 have incomplete prior
history. The earlier2,906 count established current-window availability only.
BaselineOR has284 observed entries and34 unknowns. No2026H2 volume comparison is
available. We do not compare filtered June-ending coverage with the full sample
as though missing later dates were rejected signals.

| Cohort | RVOL>1 | RVOL<=1 | Unknown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Current B09 OR | 84/136 · 61.8% | 92/148 · 62.2% | 22/34 · 64.7% |
| All B09 parents | 566/1,017 · 55.7% | 926/1,749 · 52.9% | 212/375 · 56.5% |

The current-rule difference is−0.40pp, with a95% date interval−12.40 to+12.31.
Requiring high volume would retain84 of176 winners with observed volume and
exclude92, without a higher observed hit rate. Unknowns include22 more winners;
they are not assigned a volume sign.

The all-parent pooled+2.71pp high-volume comparison has an interval−2.33 to+7.63.
Higher-volume parents also have larger prior returns and realized movement.
Comparing within the same date/hour, equally weighting blocks within dates and
then dates, yields−5.06pp [−10.05,−0.17] across115 dates and277 two-sided blocks.
This different weighting/overlap sample does not prove high volume causes harm,
and it does not justify selecting low volume as a new rule.

Within currentB09OR, only19 two-sided blocks on18 dates survive that comparison:
23 high-volume and26 ordinary-volume entries. The−17.59pp estimate has a wide
interval−42.59 to+7.41 and is too sparse for a firm directional claim. Return and
volatility balance are reported separately; no claim of fully price-matched or
causal isolation is made. The practical conclusion is simply that this single
RVOL gate did not earn an accuracy role. Transaction pressure remains a different,
untested measurement question; OHLCV does not identify buyer-initiated order flow.
