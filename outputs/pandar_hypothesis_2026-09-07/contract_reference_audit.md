## The remaining reference gap

The local chain schema contains ticker, date, expiry and strike, but **no historical option root/OSI symbol, CUSIP, deliverable components or premium/strike multipliers**. All 98 tuples match the frozen choices; that validates selection provenance, not what exercise delivers. Root-owned Theta date-specific quoted-contract lists can establish additional observed series presence. Quote presence alone is not a deliverable inventory.

The concrete authoritative route is an archived **OCC Security Definition plus Security List**, with effective updates covering each signal date through its June 18 or June 26 expiry, or an equivalent historical reference from an existing authorized provider/broker. Match exact root, expiry, right and strike; retain security identifiers, component quantities and cash, premium and strike multipliers, and settlement type. The public ENCORE DDS guide describes these full files and separate product/deliverable and series/strike updates. [OCC DDS reference](https://www.theocc.com/getcontentasset/5bb03145-905b-4d83-b647-eaa2cb076e93/dfc3d011-8f63-43f6-9ed8-4b444333a1d0/encore_dds_guide_non_proprietary_transmissions.pdf%3B).

No such dated master/archive was found in the inspected local sources. OCC documents subscriber access, but this audit did not establish historical archive availability, the user's existing entitlement, or price. Public adjustment notices are useful supporting records, not a complete negative-event inventory. Stop at this concrete gap instead of repeatedly querying prices or declaring all normal-looking roots standard. [OCC subscriber terms](https://www.theocc.com/data-subscriber-terms).
