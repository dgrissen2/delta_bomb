# Eligibility, contracts and prior richness

**50 signals across 50 current-HIRO stocks and five June 2026 entry dates passed the frozen population rules.** The original rule selected 49 call pairs; the exploratory higher-delta variant selected 47. Six variant names have completed comparable-history measurements. Population eligibility, contract selection and an executable entry are different stages; these tables establish no trade profit or final execution clearance.

The population spans five entry dates, below the protocol’s 20-date requirement for statistical support; 50 stock signals do not supply 50 independent dates.

All measurements below are tied to the signal date unless stated otherwise. Dates use the exchange session; the actual policy order can occur later in the stated entry session. All 50 signals, including failed contract selections, remain visible.

## Why each signal qualified

Admission required a valid surface and positive price/IV, **front call-wing percentile ≥85, positive absolute call wing, surface confidence ≥50%, and trailing 20-session average dollar turnover ≥$20 million**. The call wing is 5-delta 10-day IV minus ATM 10-day IV, in volatility points. Its percentile uses a 252-session window with at least 126 valid strictly prior observations. Every row also passed current-HIRO membership, next-session stock HIRO archive presence, retrospective earnings coverage and the five-session nonoverlap rule. Archive presence does not establish complete intraday data.

“Clear / clear” means no recorded earnings in either inclusive interval `[signal, signal+30 calendar days]` or `[entry, entry+30 calendar days]`, with the required metadata coverage. This is the authorized retrospective actual-event exclusion, not proof of an as-known earnings schedule. Current HIRO membership was applied backward; historical membership was not reconstructed.

## IV30 and RR30 ranks, with the pre-signal journey

**IV30 and RR30 ranks are reported descriptors, not admission thresholds in this protocol.** They are SpotGamma-compatible ORATS reconstructions, not official SpotGamma readings. Both use the share of valid observations strictly below the current value in the previous 252 exchange sessions, excluding the signal, with minimum 100 valid dates. Ties count as not below. This percentile is not a min–max IV Rank. All 50 rows have available IV30 and RR30 ranks.

RR30 is 30-day 25-delta call IV minus 25-delta put IV, in volatility points; it can be negative even with a high historical rank. “Age” is the saved episode age in sessions. No row has a left-censored age flag. “Rollover” requires two completed sessions of wing contraction; “expanding” requires two sessions of expansion; “mixed” is neither. Peak pullback is the decline from the peak known at the signal. The IV10 slope is in volatility points per session. These are descriptions of the observed journey, not fitted predictors.

## Implied versus realized volatility

IV and RV below are annualized volatility percentages. IV uses raw ORATS 30/60-calendar-day ATM summaries. RV is `sqrt(252 × mean(log return²))` over the preceding 30/60 **calendar days**, including the signal close, using reported adjusted prices and requiring every expected return. It is not a 20-trading-day Compass RV measure. The final two columns are `IV² − RV²` in annualized **decimal variance**; a positive value is descriptive, not a forecast or established edge. IV30 variance is no greater than RV30 variance for 37/50 signals; the corresponding 60-day count is 27/50. Positive variance premium was not an admission requirement.

## Exact variant-call richness: six names

History acquisition was allocated using observed preliminary entry-price/delta gates, without selecting by P&L. It covered PLTR, DIS, MRVL, ORCL, QCOM and CRM. The other 44 population rows retain an unrequested-history status; that does not mean their calls were cheap, rich or profitable. These measurements belong to the **variant far calls below**, not the original far calls above.

Current richness is exact call-bid IV minus call-mid IV interpolated at the **carry-model forward** in the same expiry. The carry estimate uses `F = S × exp((r − q − residual yield) × T)` and an internal comparison with ORATS theoretical call/put values. That consistency check does not establish an observed or independently verified forward; dividends and American exercise remain limitations. This ATM definition also differs from the 30/60-day summary ATM values above.

Each reference window contains **126 unique exchange dates strictly before the signal**. One coordinate holds call delta and calendar DTE fixed; the other holds log forward-moneyness and DTE fixed. Interpolate only within adjacent supported smiles, then interpolate bid and ATM total variance separately between bracketing expiries before subtracting their IVs. There is no extrapolation or requirement that the exact weekly contract existed throughout the window.

Each coordinate independently needs **at least 60 valid dates**. N counts dates, not strikes; standard deviation uses `ddof=1`, and percentile uses midrank ties. Missing dates occupy window slots. These histories include earnings periods and are not earnings-matched or ex-earnings histories. Both coordinates depend on the modeled-forward ATM anchor.
