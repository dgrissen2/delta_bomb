# Pandar call-leg research: seed and initial frozen specification

Created 2026-09-07, before this program requests option outcome histories. User instruction is the seed: determine whether rich-call sales and later purchases of a fixed nearer call create value, using only the HIRO stock set. Use actual events to exclude earnings in the next 30 calendar days, refresh missing earnings from ORATS, batch supported endpoints and stay within 2,000 provider attempts total. Only native Codex Charlie and Quant agents participate; these are persona simulations, not contact with the actual people.

The [index](RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS_INDEX.md) tracks three independent claims. The [decision memo](memo-pndr_pandar_call_research.md) will record each round. The [raw seed expansion](../outputs/seed_expansion.json) preserves both agents' original suggestions. This specification resolves suggestions into one executable design. Original seeds' strict as-known earnings requirement is superseded by the user's explicit authorization for retrospective actual-event exclusion. The proposed arbitrary four-stock pilot is replaced by every deterministically eligible HIRO-covered episode. Three rounds are the initial working batch, not an assertion that the user chose a numeric stopping point.

## Population, earnings and chronology

- Frozen membership: 323 stocks in [HIRO universe](../docs/replay/pandar_skew_journey_2026-09-06/hiro_universe.csv). Current membership is applied backward; this is not a survivorship-free historical universe. No ticker aliases, successor companies or adjusted option roots are substituted.
- Retain every row of the existing [signal ledger](../docs/replay/pandar_skew_journey_2026-09-06/high_rank_signal_ledger.csv), reading only pre-outcome columns. Signal range 2024-01-02 through 2026-08-05, and each entry's full 30-day event window must end by 2026-09-04. Later signals remain excluded in the ledger because their complete future event window is not yet observed.
- Signal is EOD: existing valid surface, confidence at least 50%, trailing 20-session dollar turnover at least $20m, positive prices/IV, front call-wing percentile at least 85 in a trailing 252-session window with at least 126 valid strictly prior observations, and positive absolute call wing `(5-delta 10-day IV minus ATM 10-day IV)`. A high percentile of a negative wing is insufficient.
- Exclude any actual earnings date in `[signal, signal+30 calendar days]` and `[actual short-entry date, entry+30 days]`, inclusive. Repeat at a deferred purchase. Refreshed historical events support a retrospective purge; they do not prove the future schedule was available at the order time. Empty/failed ticker histories stay unavailable. Updated-at timestamps and retrieval provenance are retained.
- Preliminary cohort requires archived individual-stock All Trades HIRO on the next exchange session. Presence is distinct from complete causal windows. Take the earliest qualifying signal per ticker, then block that ticker through its fifth holding session, including failed option selections. No replacement because a selected strike lost money or could not be traded.
- The old 2024–2026 surface results and ten reviewed option examples are exploratory. New exact-contract histories are retrospective evaluation, not pristine prospective validation. Report June results chronologically; never mix randomized train/test rows. Statistical support requires at least 20 distinct admitted entry dates. Fewer dates produce an inconclusive statistical verdict even when individual trades are profitable.

## Contracts and fixed numeric rules

All numeric strike/clock/quote gates here are **project research assumptions, not quoted Pandar requirements**. In particular, neither the old 2–6 delta band nor the new 2–10 band is claimed to come from Pandar.

1. At signal EOD choose the expiration nearest ten calendar days among expirations 7–14 calendar days away; earlier expiry wins a tie. Compute DTE from dates, not a provider's potentially inclusive DTE field.
2. Choose the OTM call with model delta 0.02–0.10 inclusive nearest 0.05; lower strike breaks a tie. Do not filter candidates by their future premium. The nearer call is the immediately adjacent lower listed strike in that expiration, still OTM at the signal. Save both identities before requesting their outcome history. No fallback expiry/strike after selection failure.
3. At the actual initial order recheck the frozen far call's 0.02–0.10 delta and positive OTM distance, remaining expiry, executable bid at least $0.20, uncrossed positive quotes, displayed size at least one on executable sides, and spread at most 30% of midpoint for both legs. Keep every rejection and observed value. Greek/model and timestamp provenance must be explicit.
4. Report delta bins `[2,5)` and `[5,10]`; OTM bins `(0,5)`, `[5,10)`, `[10,20)`, `[20,infinity)` percent; expected-move distance bins `<2`, `[2,3)`, `>=3`. Always retain continuous values. `%OTM=100*(K/S-1)` and normalized distance `log(K/F)/(ATM_IV*sqrt(T))`. These bins are descriptive, not separate optimized strategies.
5. Contract identity includes historical root, expiry, call right, strike, multiplier and deliverables. Signal-date chain and dated quoted-contract presence must agree. Split/rename/adjustment ambiguity is a rejection or unavailable executable inference, never a silent replacement. A split calendar alone does not establish OCC deliverables.

## Prior-only strike richness and journey

For the exact sold call compute executable call-bid IV minus matched ATM call-mid IV at the same expiry. ATM is interpolated at strike `K=F` inside a quoted, supported smile. Do not substitute a fitted 5-delta summary for the exact bid measurement.

Build two separately reported histories over the last 126 exchange sessions strictly before the signal: (a) the same call delta and remaining DTE; (b) the same log forward-moneyness `log(K/F)` and DTE. On each prior date interpolate only between valid adjacent quoted strikes, then between bracketing expirations in total variance. Each coordinate requires at least 60 valid **dates**, sample standard deviation with ddof=1, z-score, and midrank percentile. No extrapolation, fabricated zero wing bids, repeated strikes as extra observations, or requirement that a newly listed weekly contract itself existed for 60 days. Missing richness remains missing and is reported beside baseline replay results; it cannot count as a successful richness test.

Forward is an explicitly labeled carry-model estimate using expiry-specific ORATS rates and yields, `F=S*exp((r-q-residualYield)*T)`, interpreting the provider's residual dividend-yield adjustment additively. Check against near-ATM put/call inputs and preserve dividend/early-exercise limitations. If carry cannot be verified, the forward coordinate is unavailable; spot is not relabeled forward. [ORATS definitions](https://orats.com/docs/definitions) describe the fields.

Keep the existing pre-signal journey measures: percentile level, episode age, accumulated depth above the percentile threshold, distance from peak, two completed sessions of wing contraction, IV slope/acceleration, and 30/60-day implied variance minus realized variance. The simple primary journey contrast is two-session wing contraction versus two-session expansion at comparable initial state. Age and second differences are descriptive; do not build a fitted classifier or search thresholds.

## Entry and second-leg policies

- Clock initial entry: next session at 10:01 ET. HIRO initial-entry alternative: first qualifying five-minute decision from 10:00 through 14:30 ET; current 15-minute All Trades call flow negative, preceding 15-minute flow positive, and current underlying below the preceding 15-minute low. Action at the following minute, using information ending before the order. Missing required data is distinct from a valid no-trigger decision. Do not sum overlapping All Trades/Next Expiry/Retail groups.
- Within each initial-entry policy keep its first sale identical across all acquisition/control arms. Initial entry comparison itself is paired by eligible episode, not by matching only successful HIRO trades after the fact. A known no-trigger or known failed initial quote gate has zero trading P&L in this policy denominator; missing entry evidence is unavailable, not zero.
- Immediate spread: buy the fixed nearer call at the initial sale timestamp.
- Deferred arms: same day from the first later minute through 15:30 ET; next session 09:31–15:30; second session 09:31–15:30. Buy at the first valid quote whose ask plus one-cent adverse slippage and purchase fee is covered by the original net short-sale proceeds. Recheck current delta, distance, quote spread, size and earnings before the purchase; require nearer call still OTM and a valid positive call delta. Record values even when a purchase fails. Do not choose a cheaper strike.
- A rule that never buys retains the original short through the common exit. Do not erase the episode or call it an entered spread. Data-unavailable paths stay censored; an observed failure to trigger is a known policy outcome.
- Controls: (a) keep/cover original short at the common final deadline; (b) cover original short at the identical deferred conversion timestamp, then hold cash; (c) immediate spread. Successfully financing a spread does not establish superiority to these controls.
- Primary exit 15:50 ET on holding session four, counting sale day as session one. Session five is a prespecified secondary horizon. Cap at expiry if earlier. Identical calendar deadlines across acquisition arms from a common initial sale. Expiry/assignment handling requires verified deliverables; do not substitute theoretical settlement for a quoted close without labeling it.

## Execution accounting and inference

Use one contract, buy at ask and sell at bid, $0.65 per contract-action plus $0.01 per share adverse slippage on every action; repeat with $0.02. Preserve raw minute quotes for both legs through both deadlines, timestamps, sizes, conditions and aligned underlying/Greek snapshots. Validate quote staleness from quote-event history around orders when the interval timestamp is only a sample label. A five-second maximum age is the executable-inference gate; minute samples alone cannot satisfy it. Missing event-age/deliverable evidence permits clearly labeled quoted-price diagnostics only, not a claim that an executable trade passed all gates.

Report all initial failures, failed and uncompleted purchases, missing exits, zero-bid long marks, naked-short duration, largest observed cover loss, largest underlying rally, peak delta and worst-decile profit. A zero long bid can give a conservative mark, not evidence of a completed sale. Quote samples cannot prove the unobserved intraminute worst exposure.

Primary estimands: H-PNDR-001 HIRO initial-entry policy minus clock policy net profit per eligible episode; H-PNDR-002 next-session conversion minus immediate spread and conversion-time short cover at D4; H-PNDR-003 two-session wing rollover versus expansion net short-only profit and comparable bid-wing normalization. Require positive mean and median, at least 20 distinct dates, and a date-blocked 97.5% confidence interval above zero for each prespecified economic comparison; report multiple comparisons and insufficient samples honestly. Use 2,000 resamples of five-session blocks retaining each date's cross-section and episode grouping. Secondary timing/bins/horizons cannot rescue a failed primary claim.
