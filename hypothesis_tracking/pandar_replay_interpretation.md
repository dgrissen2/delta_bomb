# What each replay result means

The frozen rules are in [the initial protocol](pandar_seed_and_frozen_protocol.md) and [the single higher-delta variant](pandar_round2_delta_variant.md). This note explains the accounting and evidence labels; it does not change those selections.

- **Known no entry:** the specified sale failed an observed rule, or no contract/HIRO trigger qualified. Trading profit is zero in the policy denominator. This says why this particular attempted sale was skipped; it does not disqualify the ticker or the broader Pandar idea.
- **Censored entry or purchase:** missing information could change the first permitted order. Its result remains unavailable. The replay cannot jump over the gap and pick a later attractive quote.
- **Known no purchase:** every minute in the specified purchase window was checked and the fixed nearer call never met the requirements. The original short remains open until the common final cover. Its comparison with covering at the nonexistent conversion uses that same final cover, giving zero incremental difference.
- **Immediate spread unavailable:** the nearer call could not be acquired at the initial sale under the required quote/Greek rules. This comparator remains unavailable; it is not replaced by a short-only trade.
- **Zero-bid long mark:** the short can be covered, but the long has no positive selling bid. The table values that remaining long at zero and reserves a further fee/slippage allowance. This is a conservative marked result, not a completed sale of both legs.

The first sale is identical across the five purchase choices within each initial-entry policy. The clock policy sells at 10:01 ET in the session after the EOD signal. The HIRO policy acts one minute after the first fully observed qualifying flow reversal and price break. Both use the already selected far call. They are compared on the same frozen eligible episodes, including zero and unavailable outcomes.

“Same day,” “next day” and “second day” refer to trading sessions after the first sale. Each buys the already selected adjacent lower call at the first minute satisfying the financing and quote/Greek/earnings rules in its window. Financing uses the original sale proceeds after opening costs. **No later HIRO confirmation is required for the second purchase in this experiment.** Adding such a condition would require another frozen experiment and adequate later-session HIRO coverage.

Each method closes at 15:50 ET in holding session four, or session five for the secondary comparison, with the sale session counted as one. Expiry caps the deadline. The primary cost is $0.65 per contract action plus $0.01 per share of adverse slippage. The $0.02 stress keeps the primary order timestamps fixed. No margin return is computed because the short phase's capital requirement and assignment path are not established.

Minute quotes and actual quote events are separate views. The event view takes only the latest recorded NBBO event at or before an order, within a successfully captured ET session, and rejects an age greater than five seconds. It repeats this check at every candidate purchase minute, including rejected attempts. Greek and underlying observations remain joined at their exact minute timestamps and cannot come from the future. An interval sample timestamp is never used as an event timestamp.

The provider's contract tuple matches the frozen symbol, expiry, right and strike. Exact dated option deliverables and multipliers remain independently unverified. Dollar figures therefore assume a 100-share contract and are **historical quoted-price diagnostics**, not verified fills or a claim that every execution requirement has been satisfied. See the [dated contract audit](../outputs/pandar_hypothesis_2026-09-07/contract_reference_audit.md). Standard equity contracts usually represent 100 shares, but corporate actions can change terms; a standard-looking symbol alone does not settle that issue. [OCC contract specifications](https://www.theocc.com/clearance-and-settlement/clearing/equity-options-product-specifications).

All earnings exclusions use the actual historical event dates on the signal/order date through the next 30 calendar days, inclusive, as authorized by the user. They do not prove the schedule was known then. The prior richness histories match delta/DTE or forward-moneyness/DTE separately and include past earnings periods; they are not earnings-matched histories. Their ATM uses a carry-model forward with an internal model-consistency check, not an independently observed forward.

Fifty stocks do not mean fifty independent market environments. The population covers only five June entry dates. The fixed five-session resampling block therefore contains the entire date sample; repeating that block cannot establish an independent confidence interval. This remains a descriptive pilot even when individual trades earn money.
